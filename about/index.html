I’m Nathan Foale. I completed a Bachelor of Commerce at Monash University, majoring in Mathematical Econometrics and minoring in Economics. I’m now pursuing a Master of Actuarial Studies at Monash, where I’m deepening my understanding of risk, probability, financial systems and data-driven decision-making.

I’m especially interested in risk and uncertainty—how probability, simulation and data can help us understand complex systems and make better decisions. My work ranges from financial simulations and statistical analysis to interactive experiments and AI tools.

I enjoy both sides of building: developing the model underneath and designing an experience that makes the result understandable. This portfolio is a growing collection of what I’m learning, testing and building.
