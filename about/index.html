I’m Nathan Foale. I completed a Bachelor of Commerce at Monash University, majoring in Mathematical Econometrics and minoring in Economics. I’m now pursuing a Master of Actuarial Studies at Monash, where I’m deepening my understanding of risk, probability, financial systems and data-driven decision-making.

I’m drawn to questions that don’t have neat answers. Sometimes the best way to approach them is through probability, simulation and data; other times it is through history, politics and philosophy.

My academic interests bring together maths, economics and actuarial studies, with a particular focus on risk, uncertainty and complex systems. Through my projects, I explore these ideas by building models, working with data and creating interactive tools that make difficult concepts easier to understand.

My writing takes that curiosity beyond the quantitative. I write about geopolitics, political thought, philosophy, religion and the ideas that influence how people understand the world. This portfolio is a collection of the things I’m exploring, creating and gradually making sense of.
